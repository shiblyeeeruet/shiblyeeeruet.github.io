About Me

I'm a lecturer and researcher based in Gazipur, Dhaka, working at the crossroads of photonics, biosensing, and machine learning. What draws me to this field is a simple conviction: that light — manipulated precisely enough — can reveal things about the biological world that no other tool can. My work tries to bridge that gap between elegant optics and real diagnostic impact.

Beyond the lab and lecture hall, I run video lectures on the University of Scholars' official YouTube channel — making EEE concepts accessible to students wherever they are.

When I'm not thinking about sensors or syllabi, I'm probably rewatching Sherlock Holmes or Game of Thrones for the fifth time, or catching the latest Christopher Nolan film. I believe great storytelling and great research share the same core skill — the ability to make the complex feel inevitable.
